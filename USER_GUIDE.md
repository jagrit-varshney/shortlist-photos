# User Guide — shortlist-photos

A plain-English guide for family members reviewing wedding photos. No technical knowledge needed.

---

## Getting Started

### Logging in

1. Open the link your admin shared with you in any browser (phone or computer both work).
2. Enter your email and password, then tap **Sign in**.

> If you forgot your password, ask the admin to reset it for you.

---

## Home Page

After logging in you land on the home page. It shows all the photo albums (folders).

For each album you can see:
- How many photos are in it
- How many you've personally reviewed and the percentage
- A progress bar

**Buttons on each album:**
- **Resume** — appears once you've started reviewing. Takes you straight to the first photo you haven't seen yet.
- **Browse** — opens the photo grid to browse at your own pace.
- **Slideshow** — starts reviewing from the beginning of the album.

**All Photos** at the bottom lets you review every photo across all albums in one go.

---

## Reviewing Photos (Slideshow)

The slideshow shows one photo at a time, full-screen.

### The two main buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Skip** | Marks the photo as seen and moves to the next one. Does not add it to the shortlist. |
| **Shortlist ☆** | Adds the photo to the shared shortlist and moves to the next one. |

### Already shortlisted a photo?

If you tap **Shortlist** on a photo that's already in the shortlist (shown as **★ Shortlisted**), it removes it from the shortlist. You'll stay on the same photo so you can reconsider.

### The counters

- The number in the top-right (e.g. `13 / 50`) shows where you are in the current album.
- **★ 42** next to it is the total number of photos in the shared shortlist. Tap it to open the shortlist.

### Resuming later

You can stop anytime — just close the browser. Next time, tap **Resume** on the home page and you'll pick up exactly where you left off.

---

## Gallery View

Tap the **grid icon** (⊞) in the slideshow header to switch to the gallery view. This shows all photos in the album as a grid of thumbnails.

- Tap any photo to open it in the slideshow at that position.
- Shortlisted photos show a **★** badge.
- Hover over a shortlisted photo and tap **×** to remove it from the shortlist.

---

## The Shortlist Page

Tap **View shortlist →** on the home page, or tap **★** during a slideshow.

This is the **shared** shortlist — everyone's picks appear here together. You can see who selected each photo.

### Tabs

- **Shortlisted** — all photos currently in the shortlist.
- **Removed** — photos that were un-shortlisted. You can restore them.

### Removing a photo from the shortlist

Hover over a photo and tap **× Remove**. The photo moves to the **Removed** tab and can be restored anytime.

---

## Exporting the Shortlist

On the shortlist page, three export options appear in the top-right:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Export CSV** | Downloads a spreadsheet with filename, folder, who shortlisted it, and date. |
| **Export TXT** | Downloads a plain text file with one filename per line. |
| **Copy to folder** | Copies all shortlisted photo files into a new folder on the server. |

### Copy to folder

1. Tap **Copy to folder**.
2. A folder name is suggested (e.g. `shortlist_2026-06-09`). You can change it.
3. Tap **Copy**.
4. The originals are copied into a new subfolder inside the photos root directory.

> This is the easiest way to hand the final selection to a photographer — just give them access to that folder.

---

## Your Profile

Tap your name or the avatar icon in the top-right header to open your profile page. You can change your own password there.

---

## Tips

- **Multiple people can review at the same time** — everyone's progress and shortlists are tracked separately, but the shortlist itself is shared.
- **You don't need to finish an album in one go** — Resume will always bring you back to the right photo.
- **Shortlisting is not final** — anyone can add or remove photos from the shortlist at any time until you export.
